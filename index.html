Privacy Policy for Save to PDF

Effective Date: November 2026  |  Package Name: com.gsm.savetopdf

Summary: Save to PDF works 100% offline on your Android device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, documents, or usage analytics.

1. Information We Collect

None. Save to PDF does not collect, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.

2. How Print Jobs & Documents Are Processed

Save to PDF operates strictly as a local Android PrintService. When you print a webpage or document using Save to PDF:

  • The PDF stream is processed entirely in memory and saved directly to your device's local storage (such as your Downloads folder or a location you choose via Android's system file picker).
  • No document contents, file names, or metadata ever leave your device.
  • The application does not request internet access (android.permission.INTERNET) and communicates with zero external servers.

3. Storage & App Preferences

The app stores basic user preferences (such as your selected default paper size, orientation, or resolution) locally on your device using Android's standard private preferences storage. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Save to PDF contains no advertisements, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no analytics libraries.

5. Children's Privacy

Because our application does not collect any personal information from any user, it is safe for users of all ages and complies with applicable children's privacy regulations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if new features are added in future versions. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
